# New Feature Workflow

1. **Problem and evidence**
2. **Scope and non-goals**
3. **Business rules**
4. **Impact analysis**
5. **Architecture decision when required**
6. **Contract and data design**
7. **Vertical implementation**
8. **Risk-based tests**
9. **Documentation and runbook**
10. **Review and quality gates**
11. **Demo**
12. **Lesson/pattern extraction**

## Required outputs

- Feature spec.
- Acceptance criteria.
- API/event/schema changes.
- Test plan.
- Rollout/rollback note.
- Updated Decision Index or ADR for high-risk changes.
